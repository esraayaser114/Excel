## 📌 Overview

This project presents an interactive sales dashboard designed to transform sales data into meaningful business insights. It combines key performance indicators, product and country comparisons, sales channel analysis, and time-series forecasting in a single visual interface.

The dashboard helps users explore performance across different countries, products, and sales channels while examining historical revenue trends and forecasted values.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Displays total quantity and total revenue.
- **Country Performance Analysis:** Compares revenue across countries to identify high- and low-performing markets.
- **Top 5 Best-Selling Products:** Visualizes revenue generated by the leading products.
- **Sales Channel Profitability:** Compares the profit contribution of online and offline channels.
- **Revenue Trend Analysis:** Examines historical revenue over time.
- **Revenue Forecasting:** Presents forecasted revenue alongside lower and upper confidence bounds.
- **Interactive Filters:** Allows users to filter results by country, product, and sales channel.
